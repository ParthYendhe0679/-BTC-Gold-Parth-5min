Generated by `scripts/run_backtest.py` on 2026-10-03. Raw output is in `reports/*.json`.

**Bottom line:** no strategy shows a reliable positive edge on 5-minute data after
realistic costs. Before costs (the "0x" column), almost every strategy is close to
breakeven. On BTC, the 0.10% round-trip cost is larger than the average gain per trade.
The best case is Supertrend (20,3) on Gold, with +0.006% per trade, PF 1.04, and test
PF 1.09 over 76 trades. That is within noise, not a proven edge. **No 90–99% win
rate exists anywhere in these results.** The optimiser found no parameter set that
improved out-of-sample results, so all defaults are unchanged.

Method: entry at the confirmation-candle close; one position per strategy; stop and
target both inside one candle counted as a stop; gaps fill at the open; round-trip
cost deducted per trade; chronological 60/20/20 train/validation/test split.
Returns are per-trade % of price, summed without compounding.

Data: XAU/USD and BTC/USD come from Twelve Data, the bot's own feed (about 10 weeks).
BTC 6-month data comes from Binance BTCUSDT, used as a proxy.

Costs assumed: BTC 0.10% round trip (exchange taker fees plus slippage); Gold 0.02%
(about a $0.80 spread plus slippage at $4,100). Your broker's real costs decide the outcome.
